# B1 implementation resolution — contrast and family labels

Date: 2026-09-25  
Status: defined before B1 fitting or comparator output

The B1 lock refers to source-labelled favourable study families but does not enumerate the exact
aggregation. This resolution closes that ambiguity before comparator execution.

The eight favourable-labelled family units are:

1. GSE165177 successful MPTR (all 13 matched contrasts);
2. GSE246954 chemical reprogramming (2c and 7c together);
3. GSE176206 full SOKM (both cell types, ages, and experimental pools);
4. GSE297984 human chemical reprogramming (2c and 7c, both aged lines and days);
5. GSE297233 OSK/O4YRSK (both constructs);
6. GSE297234 early OSKM (96-year donor days 3 and 7 versus day 0; day 10 is trajectory-only);
7. GSE304042 ARPE OSK;
8. GSE304043 GSTA4.

These labels mean only that the source study treated the intervention as beneficial/rejuvenating or
as a nominated downstream mechanism. They are not accepted biological truth. The family effect for
a method is the unweighted mean of its complete listed contrast effects.

GSE300625 7c is the adverse family. Failed-to-reprogram GSE165177 is a negative comparator. All
other GSE176206 factor combinations and GSE297234 day 10 remain in the complete contrast matrix but
are exploratory and do not enter the favourable-family fraction. The GSE246954 untreated young-old
and GSE297234 day-0 young-old comparisons are the two age-orientation controls.
